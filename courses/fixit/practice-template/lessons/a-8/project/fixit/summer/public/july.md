# Fix-It Saturday, 10 July

**When:** Saturday 10 July 2027, 10:30am to 1pm.
**Where:** Millbrook Library, Room B (first floor). **Step-free access by lift** next to the children's library.
**Booking:** 32 places. Book on the library's events page; walk-ins at 11:00 if there's space.
**Bring:** the broken item, its charger or lead, spare parts if you have them, and a note of what goes wrong.
**We can't take:** microwaves; gas appliances; swollen or cracked batteries; jobs that need rewiring inside the casing; e-bike and e-scooter batteries.
**On the day:** one item each until 12:00. Tea is free; donations for parts welcome.
**Questions:** Millbrook Library events desk, 555-0100.
