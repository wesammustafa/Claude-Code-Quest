# Fix-It Saturday, February

**When:** Saturday 13 February 2027. Doors open at 10am, and we finish at 1pm.

**Where:** Millbrook Library, Room B. The venue is fully wheelchair accessible. Free parking on site.

**Do I need to book?** Plenty of space, just turn up!

**What to bring:** your broken item and its charger.

**What we fix:** we fix anything! Toasters, bikes, lamps, laptops, jumpers.

**What it costs:** free. Tea and biscuits provided.

**How it works:** one item per person until 12:00, then second items if a fixer is free.

**Questions?** Text Priya on 555-0187.
