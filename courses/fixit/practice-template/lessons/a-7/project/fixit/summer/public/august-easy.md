# Fix-It Saturday, 14 August, at Northside: easy read

- The Main Hall is on the ground floor. There are no steps.
- Come in by the main entrance.
- There is an accessible toilet.
- The day is Saturday 14 August 2027.
- The place is Northside Community Centre, Main Hall.
- Come from 10am. We finish at 12:30pm.
- There are 40 places. Book through the Northside office or the library's events page.
- Bring the broken thing.
- Bring its charger or lead.
- Bring spare parts if you have them.
- Bring a note of what goes wrong.
- We can't take microwaves.
- We can't take gas appliances.
- We can't take swollen or cracked batteries.
- We can't take jobs that need rewiring inside.
- We can't take e-bike or e-scooter batteries.
- We can't take lithium power tool batteries over 36 V.
- Until 12:00, bring one thing each.
- Tea is free.
- Questions? Call the Northside Community Centre office on 555-0130.
