From: Fire Safety Officer, Millbrook Library
To: Room hirers
Date: Thu 20 May 2027
Subject: Lithium batteries in meeting rooms

From 1 June 2027, groups using our meeting rooms must not accept or charge **lithium power tool batteries rated over 36 V**. Smaller batteries (phones, laptops, 18 V and 36 V tool packs) are fine. E-bike and e-scooter batteries remain excluded.
