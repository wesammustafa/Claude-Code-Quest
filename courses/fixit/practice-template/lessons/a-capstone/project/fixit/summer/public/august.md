# Fix-It Saturday, 14 August, at Northside

**When:** Saturday 14 August 2027, 10am to 12:30pm.
**Where:** Northside Community Centre, Main Hall. Level access from the main entrance; accessible toilet available.
**Booking:** 40 places. Book through the Northside office or the library's events page.
**Bring:** the broken item, its charger or lead, spare parts if you have them, and a note of what goes wrong.
**We can't take:** microwaves; gas appliances; swollen or cracked batteries; jobs that need mains rewiring inside the casing; e-bike and e-scooter batteries; lithium power tool batteries over 36 V.
**On the day:** one item each until 12:00. Tea is free.
**Questions:** Northside Community Centre office, 555-0130.
