## intro
- minutes: "about 25 minutes"
- why: "The guide is only as current as the booking you saved. The library moves groups between rooms at short notice, and nobody remembers to email you. A connection to its booking system can catch that, but a connection that can also cancel bookings is a risk the group never needed to take."
- can: "decide whether a connection earns its place over a saved file"
- can: "read what a server can do before you connect it"
- can: "add it at the right scope with a pinned version, and allow only what you need"
- can: "use it, and handle it being down without guessing"
- payoff: "use it, and handle it being down without guessing"
- read: "[Connect Claude Code to tools via MCP](https://code.claude.com/docs/en/mcp): adding a server at each scope, approving project servers, and what /mcp shows."

- option: Never, since the signed PDF is the proof
- because: "The PDF was true when it was signed. If the library moves you, it still says Room B, and nobody tells you."
- right: When it can change and you check it each month

- note: "`get_booking`: one booking, with its room, date, hours, limit, access and when it was last updated. It changes nothing."

- note: "Simulated service: in Questerm, `bookings` answers from fixed records in your browser. Nothing is sent anywhere. On your own computer you'd connect to a real system the same way."
