### W3 | Worked example step 3 of 4 | Who approves each edit
- tier: guided
- skill: plan-mode
- where: claude
- beat: do
- verify: self
- goal: "Look at the line under the input. It shows the permission mode: who approves what Claude does."
- why: "Choose who approves Claude's work before the job starts, not halfway through it."
- note: "**Auto**, `⏵⏵ auto mode on`: a safety checker approves each action, and Claude doesn't stop to ask you. Use it for small jobs you'll read afterwards anyway."
- note: "**Manual**, `⏸ manual mode on`: Claude asks before it edits a file or runs a command. Use it when you want to see each change as it happens."
- note: "**Accept edits**, `⏵⏵ accept edits on`: file edits go through without asking, and commands still ask. Use it for many small edits you'll review together at the end."
- note: "**Plan**, `⏸ plan mode on`: Claude reads and proposes a plan, and changes no files until you approve it. Use it for a job with several parts, where a wrong approach would waste time."
- beat: do
- verify: self
- goal: "Press `Shift+Tab` a few times and watch the line change. Stop on `⏸ plan mode on`."
- hint: "Each press moves to the next mode, and the line names it."
