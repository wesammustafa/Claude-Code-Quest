## intro
- minutes: "about 25 minutes"
- why: "When Maya proofread February's guide, a can't-take rule was missing, and she only noticed because someone asked at the desk. A reviewer that shares the writer's context tends to share its blind spots, and a reviewer that's wrong and trusted is worse than none."
- can: "decide when a second reviewer earns its place"
- can: "define a read-only reviewer that can't change anything"
- can: "give it the context it needs"
- can: "choose its model and effort for the job"
- can: "treat what it reports as leads you check, not verdicts you apply"
- payoff: "treat what it reports as leads you check, not verdicts you apply"
- read: "[Create custom subagents](https://code.claude.com/docs/en/sub-agents): the agent file, its tools and model, and when Claude hands a job to one."
