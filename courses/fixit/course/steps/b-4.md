### W2 | Worked example step 2 of 3 | Predict, then rewind
- tier: guided
- skill: rewind
- where: claude
- beat: predict
- ask: Claude shortened the guide, and on the way it cut lines from the library's email, one of your sources. What's the fastest safe way back?
- option: Ask Claude to put the email back the way it was
- because: "Claude would rebuild it from memory, and the original is what it just changed: you'd have nothing to check it against."
- option: Fix the email yourself from what you remember
- because: "Your memory of the email is less reliable than Claude's, and you'd still have nothing to check it against."
- right: Rewind to before that request and restore the files
- reveal: "Claude Code keeps a checkpoint before each of your messages. Rewind puts back the files Claude edited, and the conversation, exactly."
- beat: predict
- ask: You're about to rewind to before your request. Which files will come back as they were?
- option: All three: the guide and the email come back, and the backup copy goes
- because: "Rewind restores what Claude changed with its edit tool. The backup copy was made by a command Claude ran, and checkpoints don't track that."
- right: The guide and the email come back; the backup copy stays
- option: Only the guide, since the email was edited after you pressed Esc
- because: "The email was edited before you pressed `Esc`, and that edit was checkpointed like any other."
- reveal: "Edits are checkpointed; files that only a command touched aren't. You'll see this in a moment."
- beat: do
- verify: check 0 1
- goal: "With the input empty, press `Esc` twice, or type `/rewind`:"
- run: "/rewind"
- why: "Rewinding is faster than repairing: the files come back exactly as they were, with nothing rebuilt from memory."
- do: "Choose your request that starts `shorten fixit/feb/attendee-guide.md`, then choose **Restore code and conversation**."
- note: "**Restore code and conversation** puts both back. **Restore conversation** keeps the files as they are now and forgets the conversation after that point. **Restore code** puts the files back and keeps the conversation. The two **Summarize** choices shrink the conversation instead of undoing it."
- watching: fixit/feb/venue-email-feb.md and fixit/feb/attendee-guide.md
- beat: do
- where: editor
- verify: self
- goal: "Open the files tab: the guide has its eleven sections again, and the email its seven items."
- note: "`attendee-guide.backup.md` is still there, as you predicted: a command made it, so rewind didn't remove it. Your old request is back in the input, ready to edit."
