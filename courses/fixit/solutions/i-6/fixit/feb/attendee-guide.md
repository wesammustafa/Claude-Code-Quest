# Fix-It Saturday, 13 February

Bring something broken and fix it with a volunteer. Free.

**When:** Saturday 13 February 2027, 10:30am to 1pm.
**Where:** Millbrook Library, Room B, first floor.
**Getting in:** there are stairs, and a lift next to the children's library. Step-free access is being confirmed: call the library on 555-0100 before you travel.
**Parking:** the library car park is closed on Saturdays. Station Road car park is a five-minute walk.
**Booking:** 32 places. Book on the library's events page. If there is space at 11:00, we take walk-ins.
**Bring:** the broken item, its charger or power lead, any spare parts you have, and a note of what goes wrong.
**We can't take:** microwaves, gas appliances, anything with a swollen or cracked battery, or items that need mains rewiring inside the casing.
**How it works:** one item each until 12:00, then second items if a fixer is free. Tea is free; donations for parts are welcome.
**Questions:** Millbrook Library events desk, 555-0100.
