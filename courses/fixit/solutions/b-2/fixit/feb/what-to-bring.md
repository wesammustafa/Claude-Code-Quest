# What to bring to Fix-It Saturday

**Bring:**
- the broken thing
- its charger or power lead
- any spare parts you already have
- a few words on what goes wrong ("it hums but won't spin")

**Please don't bring** (safety, agreed with the library):
- microwaves
- anything that runs on gas
- anything with a swollen or cracked battery
- jobs that need rewiring inside the casing

One item per person until 12:00; after 12:00, a second item if a fixer is free.

Saturday 13 February, 10:30am to 1pm, Millbrook Library Room B.
