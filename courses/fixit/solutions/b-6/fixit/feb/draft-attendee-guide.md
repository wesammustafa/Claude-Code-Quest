# Fix-It Saturday, February

Bring something broken and fix it with a volunteer. Free.

**When:** Saturday 13 February 2027, 10:30am to 1pm. Please don't arrive before 10:30; we're setting up.

**Where:** Millbrook Library, Room B, on the first floor. There's a lift next to the children's library, and stairs.

**Parking:** the library car park is closed on Saturdays for the farmers' market. Station Road car park is a five-minute walk.

**Do I need to book?** Yes, please. We have 32 places; book on the library's events page. If there's space at 11:00, we take walk-ins.

**What to bring:** the broken item, its charger or power lead, any spare parts you already have, and a note of what goes wrong.

**What we can't take:** microwaves, gas appliances of any kind, anything with a swollen or cracked battery, or items that need mains rewiring inside the casing.

**How it works:** one item per person until 12:00, then second items if a fixer is free. Every item gets a repair card, fixed or not.

**What it costs:** nothing. Tea is free, and there's a donations jar for parts.

**Questions?** Millbrook Library events desk, 555-0100.
