# Fix-It Saturday, Saturday 10 April 2027

Bring something broken and fix it with a volunteer. Free.

**When:** Saturday 10 April 2027, 10:00am to 12:30pm.
**Where:** Northside Community Centre, Main Hall (ground floor).
**Getting in:** step-free main entrance on Grove Street, level floor throughout. **The centre's accessible toilet is being refurbished in April:** the nearest is in Millbrook Library across the road, open 9:30am to 1pm.
**Booking:** 40 places. Book on the centre's events page; walk-ins at 11:00am if there's space.
**Bring:** the broken item, its charger or power lead, any spare parts you have, and a note of what goes wrong.
**We can't take:** microwaves, gas appliances, anything with a swollen or cracked battery, jobs that need rewiring inside the casing, or e-bike and e-scooter batteries (bikes are fine without them).
**How it works:** one item each until 12:00, then second items if a fixer is free. Tea is free. Free parking in the centre's car park.
**Questions:** Northside Community Centre office, 555-0130.
