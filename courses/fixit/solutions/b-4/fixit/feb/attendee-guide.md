# Fix-It Saturday, February

Bring something broken and fix it with a volunteer. Free.

**When and where:** Saturday 13 February 2027, 10:30am to 1pm, Millbrook Library, Room B (first floor; lift next to the children's library, or stairs). Please don't arrive before 10:30.

**Parking:** the library car park and Station Road car park are both closed on 13 February. Park at the leisure centre on Park Lane (free on Saturdays), about eight minutes' walk.

**Book first:** 32 places, on the library's events page. Walk-ins at 11:00 if there's space.

**Bring:** the broken item, its charger or lead, any spare parts, and a note of what goes wrong.

**What we can't take:** microwaves, gas appliances of any kind, anything with a swollen or cracked battery, or items that need mains rewiring inside the casing.

**On the day:** one item each until 12:00. Tea is free; donations for parts welcome.

**Questions?** Millbrook Library events desk, 555-0100.
